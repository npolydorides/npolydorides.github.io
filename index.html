Welcome

Welcome to the homepage of the Computational Imaging & Data Analytics Group at the School of Engineering of the University of Edinburgh. The group is led by Prof. Nick Polydorides, Chair of Computational Engineering.

Overview

Our research group develops advanced mathematical and computational frameworks to model, image, and control complex engineering and physical systems. Our current work is structured around three core pillars:

  • Dispersion Modeling & Imaging: Advancing the computational visualization and analysis of fluid transport phenomena, specifically turbulent gas plumes and combustion.
  • Streaming Additive Manufacturing Analytics: Developing data-driven, in-situ monitoring algorithms for 3D printing processes to detect defects and analyze material deposition in real time.
  • Probabilistic Numerics for Edge Computing: Leveraging randomized numerical linear algebra (RandNLA) to significantly lower memory footprints and accelerate approximate algorithms for real-time edge devices.

We welcome inquiries from highly motivated individuals eager to bridge rigorous mathematical theory with advanced computational applications.
